## What to steal (patterns, not pixels)

From the Awwwards labels + posters:
- Knowledge → activation narrative: hero states the unification promise, product pages prove it.
- Card-based product tour (3up), large rounded screenshots, soft shadows.
- Contact-as-popup, not a page – low friction conversion.
- Dense but airy footer – sitemap + social + legal in one band.
- Short looping showcase videos for motion – use your own screen recordings instead.

Rebuild with your own copy, stock/LLM-generated placeholders, and your brand tokens.
If you need the actual Tildei assets for a license, contact the site owner via Awwwards.
