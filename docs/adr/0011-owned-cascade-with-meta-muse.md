# An owned cascade: Meta for ear and mind, AssemblyAI for the voice

The managed AssemblyAI Voice Agent owns the two things this product needs to own: **when a turn ends** and **which model answers**. That ownership is not a preference we can negotiate — the wire protocol offers no way to commit a turn early, no visibility of the model behind a tenant call, and no lever on the reply gap. And the reply gap is the one budget that fails: `replyGapP50Ms` is frozen at 1500 with `replyGapP95Ms` at 2500, and the managed path measures 1923 p50 against it. Four gateway models, every streaming-STT preset and three turn-window rungs all landed between 1.9 s and 2.3 s. The limiting term is not ours to move while the vendor owns the boundary.

Two facts make owning it cheaper than repairing it. The vendor's own text-to-speech is reachable directly — `POST https://tts.us.assemblyai.com/v1/speech` and `wss://tts.us.assemblyai.com/v1/ws` on `assemblyai-tts-1` — and our own evidence was captured through it: 15 of our 16 voices render, with `pcm_s16le` at 24 kHz native for the browser and `pcm_mulaw` at 8 kHz native for the phone, plus `Cancel` and `Flush` that the Voice Agent API never exposed to us. The app currently asserts this endpoint does not exist (`api/voice-preview/route.ts`), which is a belief we can now drop. And Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe does native endpointing, in 25 languages, with vocabulary biasing, on a model we can call ourselves.

So we own the orchestration. Speech-to-text defaults to Meta `muse-voice-transcribe-1.0` in `ENDPOINTING` mode with AssemblyAI `universal-3-6-pro` behind a breaker. The LLM defaults to Meta `muse-spark-1.3` in **Standard** mode with a gateway model behind the same kind of breaker. Synthesis is AssemblyAI `assemblyai-tts-1` and nothing else, because there is no second voice worth having and no Meta TTS at all. The browser transport (24 kHz `pcm_s16le`) and the Telnyx transport (8 kHz `pcm_mulaw`) are both specified; only the browser ships in the first slice.

One correction to the original premise has to be recorded rather than discovered later. **Meta has no per-turn commit.** Its realtime socket accepts exactly one client text frame after the handshake — `endStream`, which ends the *session*, not a turn — and `PUSH_TO_TALK` is "single-turn and the client delimits the turn by ending the stream". The cascade was scoped to replace turn-based waiting with early commit; that is not shippable against Meta, in any mode. The turn boundary therefore belongs to the provider's endpointing, and our turn gate is demoted to **floor arbitration**: while the agent holds the floor, it decides interruption, backchannel, or wait. The latency win that remains is real but smaller — a pre-opened synthesis session, a sub-sentence first chunk, and speculation against the growing partial.

The managed path stays the routing default until the cascade is proven on the frozen budgets for two consecutive rounds, and it is never a mid-call handover: it is an alternative chosen before the first frame.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the managed path and tune it.** Rejected. It was tuned: four models, every STT preset, three turn-window rungs. Every result sat between 1.9 s and 2.3 s, which is what a boundary we do not own looks like. A fifth knob is a fourth failed experiment.
- **Couple the providers in pairs** — Meta STT+LLM, then AssemblyAI STT+gateway LLM, then managed. Rejected. A dropped speech socket would demote a healthy model for a reason unrelated to the failure, and "which model answered this turn" would become a function of an unrelated outage.
- **Use the Contributor model tier.** Rejected. It is 12.5×/21× cheaper and saves roughly $2.3k/month at 100k calls, but its basis is permission to train Meta models on the content we send — which on this product is a caller's name, budget, financing and phone number. Standard also carries 3,000 RPM against Contributor's 150, which removes the prefetch squeeze rather than engineering around it.
- **Speculate a commit at `speechEnd`.** Deferred, not rejected. The post-processing gap between `speechEnd` and `speechComplete` is undocumented, the final transcript may differ from the last partial, and a divergence costs a second LLM request. If the measured reply gap misses after cutover, this becomes its own ticket with its own evidence.
- **A cascade-specific agent body.** Rejected. It recreates exactly the two-builders divergence ADR-0006 ended. The cascade reads the same fingerprinted body through one adapter with an explicit field map and a failing ignore list.
- **Ship the phone path first.** Rejected. The phone has no delivered-audio instrument (ADR-0007), and ADR-0009 forbids a quality claim that is not Delivered audio. A phone-first cutover would be a claim we cannot measure.
- **Keep the voice-per-locale catalogue unchanged.** Rejected for one voice. `juergen` returns `422 voice_not_found` from the TTS endpoint, so German cannot move until it is re-cast and auditioned.

## Consequences

- **The instrument loses a leg.** A cascade call has no platform recording, so W0 runs two captures — reference and delivered — instead of three. The verdict keeps its value: reference-versus-delivered still separates the vendor's voice from our pipeline, and the model's text becomes directly observable in our own transcript rather than inferred.
- **`output.volume` becomes managed-only.** Direct synthesis has no such parameter. The measurement already says no voice needs gain — every measured voice is within 2.4 LU of the reference with `volume: 100` — so the field is retired from the cascade instead of being re-implemented as a server-side stage nobody asked for.
- **`turn_detection` stops meaning anything on the cascade.** There is no turn-detection concept to set. Tickets 64 and 65 remain true for the managed path while it lives and close with it.
- **The greeting has to be rebuilt.** The cascade currently opens in silence until the visitor speaks. The body's greeting must be spoken before any user audio, through the bound voice, cancellable by barge-in. Without it, the reference line and the audition clips describe audio the call never says.
- **German is re-cast.** One voice fails on the synthesis path; the Locale is servable by catalogue voices. The re-cast is the owner's ear, recorded with a named reviewer and a date, and German enters the same gate as every other subject.
- **The privacy surface grows from one vendor to four** — AssemblyAI, Meta, and Vercel/Google on the fallback path. Each needs a disclosure line, zero data retention is requested for raw audio, and the Meta handshake is a credential inside a JSON body, so request-body logging joins the denylist.
- **A second operational surface.** The Next app deploys with `wrangler deploy`; a Python process that holds a three-minute socket cannot live on Workers. That is a new host, a new drain-on-deploy rule, and a new pager.
- **A run that fell back is not evidence.** Any fallback, or a budget stop, makes a run ineligible for green. Otherwise a green number can be produced by a stack that is not the one being cut over.
- **The kill criterion is stated in advance.** Three measured rounds without `replyGapP50Ms ≤ 1500` ends the work and keeps the managed path. The direction already said managed stays until beaten; this makes it a decision rather than a vigil.
